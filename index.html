Robust Near-Field Millimeter-Level Positioning and Navigation Method Based on High-Q Factor Modes in PT-Symmetric System

TIE 2025


Abstract

For the instability errors such as clock asynchronization and multipath effect that exist in far-field positioning techniques, this paper constructs a robust high-precision positioning and navigation control method at eigen-frequency, by utilizing the transmission directionality and real-time response characteristics of magnetic resonance near-field parity-time (PT) symmetry. Firstly, based on the coupled mode theory, the quantitative relationship between the coupling rate and the gain/loss parameters in the high Q-factor mode is given. On this basis, using the exact correspondence property between the relative distance of transmitter-receiver and the coupling rate, the exact correspondence function between the gain modulation quantity and the relative distance is established. Then, the significant sensitivity of the received strength to the change of spatial position is further revealed, thus the robustness correspondence between the position of high Q-factor mode and the maximum received strength is confirmed, and this feature effectively improves the recognizability of signal detection. Based on this, this mode position is taken as the target position, and a method of tracking the maximum received intensity to realize the target position adjustment is proposed. Finally, physical experiment based on an unmanned vehicle is employed to validate the effectiveness of the proposed near-field positioning and navigation methodology.
